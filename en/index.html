Android and web

01 — Android · Free

Matvaretabellen

Calorie counter and nutrition lookup built on the official Norwegian food composition table. No account – everything stays on your phone.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy

Features

  • Search more than 2,000 Norwegian foods, sort and filter by nutrient
  • Scan the barcode on packaged foods
  • Set a calorie goal and log what you eat, with protein, carbs and fat
  • Build your own meals and add your own foods
  • History, calendar and trends over 7, 30 and 90 days
  • Vitamins and minerals against recommended intake (NNR 2023)
  • Compare foods side by side
  • Works offline

02 — Android · Free

Today: Sun, Weather & Facts

Everything about today, for the place you are.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy

Features

  • Sunrise, sunset and day length
  • Twilight, moon phase and moonrise
  • Weather and hourly forecast from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute
  • Name days and observances
  • What happened on this date in history
  • Quote of the day
  • Sun and moon are calculated on the phone and work offline

03 — Web · Free

WattsLeft

A simulator for cycling team time trials and paceline riding. Enter riders, route and weather, and see who should pull for how long – and who cracks. During the ride the team follows the pull plan live on their phones, outdoors or on Zwift.

Open WattsLeft

Features

  • Riders with weight, FTP and training level
  • Import the route as GPX, or pick a Zwift route
  • Weather forecast along the route, with wind and air density
  • Pull lengths and pace worked out from the physics
  • Replay of the ride, with paceline and map
  • Live session: a shared countdown on every phone showing who is on the front, seconds left and who is next
  • Compare strategies, such as whether to sacrifice a rider
  • Uncertainty range around the finish time
