Privacy policy

Matvaretabellen

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: I do not collect information about you. What you enter in the app stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google, and some features fetch data from the internet, as described below.

What you enter in the app

Favourites, meals, custom foods, calorie goals, your profile (age and sex, used for recommended daily intake) and your food log are stored only on your phone. The app has no account or sign-up, and none of this is sent to me.

Backup and export

The app uses Android's built-in backup, so your data can move with you to a new phone. The backup is stored in your Google account, and you control it in your phone's settings. The food table itself is not included in the backup.

Export to a file only happens when you ask for it, and you choose where the file goes.

Data fetched from the internet

  • Food data is fetched from matvaretabellen.no. No personal data is sent, but as with any web request the server sees your IP address.
  • Barcode scanning: The camera is only used while the scanner is open. The barcode is read on the phone with Google ML Kit, and the image is neither stored nor sent. The barcode number itself is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Google may collect anonymous usage statistics about ML Kit.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. In the EEA the app asks for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later under privacy options in the app.

Read more in Google's privacy policy and about how Google uses data from apps.

Purchasing “Remove ads”

The purchase is handled by Google Play. I receive no payment details, only a confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can hide the ads.

Crash reports

The app has no analytics or tracking of its own. If you have turned on sharing of usage and diagnostics data in Android, Google Play may give me anonymised crash reports and performance figures.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

Your rights

Since I hold no information about you, there is nothing for me to show you or delete. You remove everything the app has stored by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings under Google › Ads.

If you believe personal data is being processed unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Contact

The app is published by Rolv Råen under the name Strong Resolve. Questions about privacy can be sent to hei@strongresolve.no.

Changes

If I change this policy, the date at the top of the page is updated.
