Privacy policy

Today: Sun, Weather & Facts

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: I do not collect information about you. Your location is used to calculate sun and moon on the phone and to fetch the weather from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute. The app shows ads from Google.

Location

The app asks for access to your location. You choose whether it is precise or approximate. Sunrise, sunset, twilight and the moon are calculated on the phone.

To fetch the weather, the coordinates are sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no and frost.met.no). Your location is not sent to me and is only stored temporarily on the phone. If you do not grant access, Oslo is used.

Other data from the internet

Historical events are fetched from Wikipedia via Wikimedia. Only the date and language are sent. Name days, observances and quotes are built into the app.

Settings and backup

Your settings are stored on the phone and may be included in Android's built-in backup to your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads.

Read more in Google's privacy policy and about how Google uses data from apps.

Purchasing “Remove ads”

The purchase is handled by Google Play. I receive no payment details, only a confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can hide the ads.

Crash reports

The app has no analytics or tracking of its own. If you have turned on sharing of usage and diagnostics data in Android, Google Play may give me anonymised crash reports and performance figures.

Sources and licences

  • Weather: Norwegian Meteorological Institute, NLOD and CC BY 4.0
  • Historical events: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0
  • Sun and moon: NOAA solar equations, calculated on the phone

Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

Your rights

Since I hold no information about you, there is nothing for me to show you or delete. You remove everything the app has stored by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings under Google › Ads.

If you believe personal data is being processed unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Contact

The app is published by Rolv Råen under the name Strong Resolve. Questions about privacy can be sent to hei@strongresolve.no.

Changes

If I change this policy, the date at the top of the page is updated.
